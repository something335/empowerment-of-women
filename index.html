The Empowerment of Women in Rural areas

12.08.2026

WRITTEN BY: Aahana kumar

THE REALITY OF OUR WORLD

Many people believe that recent laws and decrees have revolutionized the way the world looks at women. This is undoubtedly true. But it is a generalization of our surroundings.

Observing women’s treatment here is much more different compared to our bustling city life isn’t it? And yet here we see similar day to day routines. People working, eating, washing and sleeping. But there is one major difference… where we see a woman CEO we see a man collecting water. Where we see a woman setting laws and the fate of a criminal we see a man gathering sugarcane. But why? There is 1 highly logical answer to that… It is simply that with 50% of our population cleaning houses and feeding kids we are struggling to make progress in economy, business expansion and social awareness.

But before we go about forcing them to try out for jobs we must ask ourselves why they didn’t stand up for themselves in the first place.

THE REASON

Different people have different ideologies and beliefs about the world and it is important to understand each of them with care. Some of the most common reasons are:

Tradition: Many beliefs state that women are lower than men. This of course isn’t true but women in the thought of respect refuse to deny it.

Lack of education: Ever since childhood, girls and women never got proper education. They weren’t aware that they were being treated unfairly as they were taught to consider male the supreme gender.

Domestic abuse: Women who do know about the ongoings however fear that should they speak up the men would attack them physically.

Unfortunately Africa isn’t the only home to disregarded women. Hundreds of other villages across the globe.

THE SOLUTION

Fortunately there is something we citizens can do to build confidence in women. We can educate them on basic rights and global languages. We can hire middle age women to do simple and self-explanatory jobs. As for senior citizens we can convince the government to provide satisfactory loans to them as they are not in shape to work.

This way women will not only get more opportunities and choices in life but they will also learn an important lesson… women deserve the equivalent of men. And should they not get it they don’t need to hesitate in voicing out their opinion.
